Your best operator's knowledge stays at the plant

Every plant runs on things that aren't in the O&M manual: which pump to favor in wet weather, what the DO probe does when it's going bad, when to waste harder in the spring. That knowledge lives in a few people's heads, and it leaves when they retire or move on. Tailwater gives it a place to stay.

SME rules, in your own words

Your senior operator writes the rule like they'd tell a new hire. Their words are kept exactly. Tailwater makes a plainer version to help find the rule, and the author checks that it still means the same thing.

Who wrote it and where it applies

Every rule is stored with its author. A rule can apply to one plant or all of your plants, and old rules are retired, not lost.

What affects what

Cause and effect, written as sentences: when this reading goes up, that one moves, and here is why. The what-if screen on the plant drawing uses these rules to show a trainee what a change does downstream.

Plant Library

Drawings and schematics, SOPs and photos, each with a caption, kept with the plant so they're there when someone needs them.

Ask your data

Ask a question in plain English. Answers cite the reading and the time it was taken, and your plant's SME rules come first.

A certified operator decides

Tailwater recommends; a certified operator reviews before acting. Saving a rule records reviewed human guidance. It doesn't train a model on your data.
