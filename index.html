What Tailwater does

Made for operators and plant managers, so the daily work goes quick and the EPA paperwork is ready when it's due.

Permit limits and eDMR

Bench sheet columns carry the same locations and STORET codes as your permit. Each limit is checked against your data, and missing samples are flagged before the month closes.

Sampling calendar

Required samples are laid out on your lab's pickup days, so a sample doesn't get missed late in the period.

Lab and field data

Enter bench sheets and field readings once, and see trends, limits and history without digging through files.

Plant diagram and levels

See your process on a plant diagram, including tank levels and live readings where a SCADA connection is available.

Maintenance work orders (CMMS)

Equipment list, scheduled and corrective work orders, and maintenance history in one place. Built to meet the EPA asset management and CMMS requirements for your system.

Pretreatment and biosolids

Track industrial users, sampling and limits for your pretreatment program, and biosolids quantities, analyses and land application records. Reports come straight from the data you already entered.

Rounds and operator notes

Operators log rounds and shift notes in the program, so the next shift sees what was checked, what was found and what still needs attention.

What affects what

See how your process readings tie together, so you can trace what a change at one point does to the rest of the plant.

Graph Studio

Build your own trend charts from any readings you track, side by side, and save them for the next time you need them.

Ask your data

A built-in assistant answers questions from your plant's own records, and your experienced staff can teach it the local rules.

We set it up for you

We pull your permit from the EPA and build your bench sheets, permit limits, sampling calendar, plant diagram and equipment list. You review it with us before you go live.

Your best operator's knowledge stays at the plant

Every plant runs on things that aren't in the O&M manual: which pump to favor in wet weather, what the DO probe does when it's going bad, when to waste harder in the spring. That knowledge lives in a few people's heads, and it leaves when they retire or move on.

Your SME writes the rule

Your senior operator or plant manager adds a rule in plain words, like they'd tell a new hire. Only people you authorize can add or approve rules.

Attach what backs it up

Drawings, schematics, SOPs and photos go in with a caption and are tied to the equipment on the plant diagram, so they're one click away where they apply.

Everyone gets the answer

When an operator asks Tailwater a question, it answers from your plant's own rules and records first, not generic textbook advice.

It gets smarter about your plant

Each rule your people add makes Tailwater fit your plant better. A new operator on night shift gets the same answer your 30-year operator would give.
