Compliance and reporting

The paperwork, ready before it is due.

Permit limits and violations

Each permit limit is checked against your data every month. Missing samples and exceedances are flagged before you file.

Sampling calendar

Reads every limit that has a monitoring frequency, works out how many samples the month owes, and puts them on your lab's pickup days.

Monthly reports and eDMR

The monthly filing comes first. Tailwater builds the export for your state's eDMR and the other reports you owe an agency.

Biosolids

Batches, fields and loads out, checked against the 40 CFR 503 ceilings, and the annual biosolids report built from what you logged.

Pretreatment

Your industrial users, their permits and limits. Significant non-compliance is worked out from the last six months of results, and permits expiring inside 90 days are flagged.

Surcharges

Set your sewer-use ordinance rates and Tailwater figures what each industry owes for strength over the domestic allowance.
